Dumb Boxing Timer — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Dumb Boxing Timer does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal or usage data.

The app has no internet access, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.

Your timer settings (round length, rest, sounds, font, language) are saved only on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

The app uses one permission: vibration, to vibrate with timer signals when you turn this option on.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
